## About Nur Islam

I am **Nur Islam**, a Dhaka-based **CTO, Founder & CEO, Linux system administrator, and DevOps engineer** with **10+ years** shipping production infrastructure and software.

At **[SOFT HAB](https://www.softhab.com)** I lead technology strategy, engineering, and cloud delivery for POS, ERP, SaaS, mobile, and AI-powered platforms. At **[HOST HAB](https://hosthab.com)** — the company I founded — I build and operate **fast, secure web hosting in Bangladesh**: cPanel shared hosting, reseller hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, and managed WordPress, with a **99.99% uptime** focus.

My day-to-day work sits at the intersection of **server administration, cloud architecture, WHMCS automation, WordPress, Laravel, and full-stack product engineering**. Clients trust the stack because it stays online, stays secure, and stays simple to run.

- Currently leading product and infrastructure at **SOFT HAB** and **HOST HAB**
- Open to collaboration on **hosting automation, WHMCS gateways, cloud migrations, and high-availability Linux stacks**
- Ask me about **cPanel/WHM, AWS, SSL, WHMCS, WordPress, Laravel, Nginx, Cloudflare, and CI/CD**
- Based in **Kawran Bazar, Dhaka** — working with teams across Bangladesh and remotely worldwide
